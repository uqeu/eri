# eri — Positioning & Messaging

The source of truth for how we talk about eri. Honest (pre-launch alpha, zero
fake metrics), on-brand (dark-only, Funnel Display, "eri" wordmark), and we say
**adaptive / predictive**, never literally "AGI."

---

## The one-liner
**eri is the agent that becomes your computer — talk to it, look at it, and it
does the rest.**

Alt, shorter: **Your computer, hands-free.**

## The 10-second pitch
eri is a personalized AI that runs *with* you on your machine. You speak, you
glance, and eri acts — opens apps, writes code, navigates the web, handles the
busywork — while learning how you work so it gets sharper every day. Voice, gaze,
and memory stay **on your device**; the heavy thinking runs in the cloud only
when it's worth it. One app for everything you do on a computer.

## The manifesto (for an About / hero scroll)
> Software still makes you do the work — clicking, typing, hunting through menus,
> repeating yourself.
>
> eri flips it. You tell it what you want, or just look at the thing you mean,
> and it happens. It watches your screen (locally, privately), remembers what
> matters to you, and acts — across every app, not just one.
>
> It's a coding agent, a computer-use agent, and a second pair of hands, in one.
> The more you use it, the more it's *yours*.
>
> Adaptive. Personal. Hands-free. This is what using a computer should feel like.

## Capability pillars (benefit-first copy)
Lead with what the user *gets*, not the internals.

**Control anything, hands-free.**
"Open Discord, search YouTube, fix this file, join the call." Say it or point with
your eyes — eri operates your real apps: mouse, keyboard, windows. It asks before
anything destructive, and a single hotkey stops it cold.

**It sees what you see.**
eri understands what's on your screen — the error, the form, the design — so you
never re-explain. Faces are blurred on-device before anything is ever sent out.

**Talk naturally; it writes it clean.**
Dictate anywhere. eri fixes the grammar, learns your vocabulary, expands your
snippets, and turns rambling into polished text — and you can just say "fix that
word."

**It remembers you.**
eri keeps a private, on-device memory of how you work — your projects, habits,
preferences — and anticipates. "You usually open this next — want me to?"

**It builds, too.**
A full coding agent: plans, edits across files, runs your tests, ships. The same
assistant that runs your computer also writes your software.

**Private by design.**
Voice, gaze, and memory live on your machine. The cloud is used only when it
genuinely helps — and you always see what left the device.

## Differentiators (the "why eri, not X")
- **One app, not ten.** Coding agent + computer-use + dictation + memory in a
  single tool that works across everything — not a single-app copilot.
- **Local-first moat.** Eyes, voice, and memory are on-device. That's privacy
  *and* speed *and* lower cost — by design, not as a setting.
- **Token-frugal.** eri reads the cheap signals first and only pays for the
  expensive model when it's worth it, so you get more for your plan.
- **It's yours.** Personalization that compounds — eri adapts to you, it doesn't
  make you adapt to it.
- **Safe to hand the keys.** Confirmation gates on anything risky, a panic stop,
  and nothing destructive happens silently.

## Voice & tone
Confident, warm, plain-spoken. Short sentences. Show, don't boast. Technical
when it earns trust (local-first, on-device), human everywhere else. Lowercase
"eri" always. Never hype we can't back; never "AGI"; never invented numbers.

## Honest-framing rules (non-negotiable)
- Pre-launch: "early access," "alpha," "join the waitlist" — not finished-product
  language.
- No testimonials, no user/revenue/▲% metrics until they're real.
- "Adaptive / predictive / personalized," never "AGI."
- Don't claim a capability that isn't shipped + verified; "coming" is fine when
  labeled.
