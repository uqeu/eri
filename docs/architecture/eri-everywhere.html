One mind, everywhere you are.

eri is an adaptive personal intelligence that lives in the cloud, talks to you where you already are, and acts on your apps and your computer — logging everything, undoing anything reversible, stopping before anything costly. The promise

The marketing site makes a specific contract. The product has to actually do it — these are the load-bearing claims.

Runs your computer

Ships code, clears the inbox, fixes sheets, queues music — by API where it can, by driving the real app on-screen where it can't.

Comes with you

Texts you over iMessage (and soon WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord). One mind, same memory, across every surface.

Knows you

A private, compounding model of you — your people, habits, voice — that grows over time and syncs across devices.

Shows its work

A live thought-stream: eri streams its thinking and every action it takes. The trust surface, not a black box.

Stays safe

Every action logged and undoable. Confirms before anything costly. Passwords, payments, CAPTCHAs are a hard boundary.

Adapts to you

Capabilities stay the same; the judgment behind them becomes more “yours” — per-user, and per-group.

Three pillars

Where you reach eri, how eri acts, and the fallback that means it always can.

Spectrum · interfaces

Where you talk to eri

Photon/Spectrum gives eri managed lines and a unified message stream: iMessage (live), then WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, and Discord (custom adapter). Rich content — voice, attachments, polls, mini-app cards — with adaptive per-platform rendering.

Composio · actions

How eri acts on your apps

The 1,000+ app connector layer. When an app is connected, eri calls its API directly — fast, precise, structured. The whole catalogue is reachable today via the Tool Router; headline apps get crafted, polished flows.

Computer control · fallback

When there's no API

eri lives inside your computer and drives the real app: sees the screen, moves the mouse, types, reads the result, keeps going. Connect it and eri's faster; don't, and eri still does it by hand.

The world model

eri isn't a chatbot with memory — it's a growing model of one person (and, in groups, of one room).

Of you

People, habits, projects, preferences, voice — a private, compounding representation that informs every judgment, not just a fact lookup.

Of the group

When eri joins a group chat it seeds from whoever added it, then learns the room's collective register and phases to match — hype room, hype eri; calm room, calm eri.

Of the moment

Live context — where you are, what's on your calendar, what you just said — fused with the durable model so the proactive nudge lands at the right time.

Decisions on record

The calls we grilled and locked, so we don't relitigate them.

Platforms
iMessage / WhatsApp / Telegram / Slack native + Discord adapter. Instagram is an action target (Composio / computer-use), not an interface.
Hosting
Vercel webhooks for conversation; one small always-on worker for real-time location + deep streams.
Identity
One shared eri line; people told apart by handle; auto-allowlist on phone-link. Dedicated number (Business) later.
Voice
Text by default; voice replies a per-user setting (ElevenLabs). Incoming voice transcribed with Whisper (free).
Mini-apps
No-install first (polls + web-cards) for everyone; native installable extension later as a premium layer.
Brain
Claude now, behind a swappable seam; route each task to the cheapest model that clears the bar. No custom-trained model.
Action path
API first → computer-control fallback. Connect it and eri's faster; don't, and eri still does it.
Not doing
LiveContainer (on-device iOS app launcher) — nothing for it to run in a server relay.
