eri - Architecture & Vision Reference

The single source of truth for what eri is and how it's built: the one brain, every surface, the safety spine, and the roadmap to the digital human. Read this first; never re-derive it. Last updated 2026-06-25.

What   eri = Engineered Relative Intelligence - the AI operating system for a person; your "second digital half"
Company   Eri Labs - an AGI lab; the product is honest, the mission is bold
One brain   one world model of you, reached from your computer and your phone; it learns, predicts, and acts
Base engine   Claude (the cortex / muscle, + drives your other coding agents) + a small local model (token-free low-level) + Mercury (fast code edits)

1North star

eri is one digital human you create to your liking - a world model of you (your people, projects, habits, voice, life) that lives with you across devices, learns continuously, predicts what you'll want, and acts on your behalf. The posture is an AGI lab whose product stays honest: we say "adaptive / learns you / your other half", never literal present-tense "AGI" or "sentient" (legal + credibility). "AGI grows one person at a time."

knows you

World model

A persistent, bi-temporal graph of your world (people / projects / observations / predictions) with provenance. The top concept; memory is one component of it. §3

acts for you

Computer control

Hands-free eyes + voice that drive your actual computer, gated by a confirmation envelope so it's safe. The launch wedge. §8

grows with you

sia loop

It learns from outcomes (undo = "ask next time"), admitting only changes that earn their keep, and can only make itself more careful. §5

One brain, three surfaces. There is exactly one canonical brain (Rust, crates/). The computer app, the phone (iMessage), and the website are windows into it - never separate brains. A future cloud-resident enclave (ADR-0008) is where the brain ultimately lives so it serves you with the laptop off. Build (the old "AI IDE") is now one capability of this brain, not the product (§10, ADR-0009).

3The world model shipped (P1)

The brain is one graph (crates/eri_graph, ADR-0007). Everything - entities, relations, observations, learned action-chains - is nodes and edges in a single store, so the model of you is unified, not scattered across tables.

What it holds

  • Entities - people / projects / apps, salience-ranked by evidence count.
  • Relations - collaborates_on, related, … typed edges between entities.
  • Observations - the raw "what happened", with provenance back to source.
  • Chains - learned "after X you usually Y" with weighted NEXT edges (predictions).

How it stays honest

  • Bi-temporal - valid_from/valid_to; facts are invalidated, not deleted, so history is auditable.
  • Provenance - every node/edge carries its source (observed vs. reflection vs. sia); inferred ≠ observed.
  • Typo-tolerant resolution - OSA edit-distance entity merge, conservative, so "Priya" / "priya" don't fork.

How it fills: reflection shipped

Ingest stays zero-token and deterministic (CueExtractor, the hot path). A separate cold-path reflection pass (ADR-0007 §3, eri memory reflect) batches recent observations → one base-engine call → structured entities/relations → gated into the graph. Defensive over untrusted model output: malformed JSON → empty, unknown kinds dropped, dangling relations dropped, grounded (an entity named in no source text is dropped - no fabrication), provenance source="reflection". Seam lives in eri_inference::memory; eri_cli never names eri_graph in prod.

4Predict → gate → act shipped (P2)

Every action eri takes passes through a confirmation envelope (ADR-0006). The brain predicts a confidence, classifies reversibility, and the gate decides: auto-execute only when reversible & confident & known; anything irreversible, sensitive, or novel always asks.

action proposed→ predict confidence(model self-report + world-model forecast + cross-turn corroboration)
gatedenylist → refusal (absolute) → caution → grant → confidence floor→ auto-run/hold for confirm/refuse
  • Reversibility taxonomy + clamped confidence; the predict step is one deep module (predict::confidence_for over a WorldView handle - the opened world graph + cross-turn cursor as one value).
  • Three independent axes, never conflated: the refusal boundary (absolute), the grant axis (explicit or sia-earned, always announced), and the chain/confidence axis (what sia refines). §6
  • Files: crates/eri_cli/src/{control_envelope,predict,control_agent,control_safety}.rs. One unified gate (gate_and_confirm) across the main, coding, and fast-lane paths - no duplicated confirm logic.

5The sia learning loop shipped (P3)

eri gets better from its own outcomes - no weight-training in v1 (ADR-0007 §5): it refines world-model / chain / confidence state only. The loop is closed end-to-end:

observe→propose→ admit (earns-its-keep)→apply→ measure→keep / revert
StageWhat
observeone typed Outcome stream (AutoExecuted / Confirmed / Declined / Refused / Undone) through the existing ControlTurnSink - consolidated, not a parallel sink.
admitsia::accept - a pure gate: re-checks the canonical classify_refusal (no copy), requires support ≥ 2 (a one-off never changes policy), stamps provenance source="sia", bi-temporal so it's reversible.
applyonly via RefinablePolicy - a narrow type with exactly three ops (raise floor / downweight chain / caution). It cannot name the refusal list or widen a grant: the hard safety line is enforced by the type, not by convention.
measurecalibrate - did the change actually reduce repeat undos? keep / revert (bi-temporal supersede). Confounded signals (two mechanisms on one pattern) are left alone, never falsely credited.
THE invariant - sia only tightens. Floors only rise (never below the default), chains only weaken, cautions only add. No sequence of accepted refinements can make the gate auto-execute something it wouldn't have before. sia can only make eri more careful, never more reckless - proven by test.

Honest state: the loop is a complete, safe machine, but its observable prod effect is currently small - the undo signal is largely covered by the pre-existing always-ask caution, so the floor mechanism waits for finer signals the caution doesn't cover (genuine future work). Files: crates/eri_cli/src/sia.rs.

6The safety spine

This is the part we protect hardest. It is the difference between a loyal digital half and a liability.

absolute

Refusal boundary

Credentials, money movement, CAPTCHAs, and access-control changes are never grantable - not by the user, not by sia, not by any observed instruction. Checked last + independently, never widened. control_safety::REFUSAL_PATTERNS / classify_refusal is the one source of truth.

single principal

Loyalty

eri serves one person. No third-party egress; instructions found in observed content (web pages, emails, docs) are data, not commands. Loyalty ≠ blind obedience - it stays honest with you.

confidential

The core stays private

The full world model is confidential: E2E (K_user) today → an operator-can't-read enclave at the ADR-0008 end-state. Only a small, gated projection is ever server-readable. §7

alive-but-honest

Disclosure

eri presents as real, never as biological-human; it discloses it's an AI and never optimizes to deceive that it's a person. Goes in the ToS.

Privacy zones (Q23) - health, financial, intimate, and credential content - are filtered before anything leaves the confidential boundary, fail-closed (over-exclusion is acceptable). Loyalty egress (Q24) - any projection goes only to the user's own devices, keyed to user_id. These two gates are what a future social layer must extend deliberately, never bolt past.

7Recall seam - brain ↔ phone brain half shipped (ADR-0010)

The bridge that makes the world model show up when you text eri. The serverless phone cannot call the local Rust brain live, so the brain pre-computes a bounded, privacy-gated projection and pushes it to the server-readable store the phone already reads. The phone does one cheap DB read.

Rust brainrecall_projection(user_id, recipient) → gate (fail-closed)push (Tier-A sync) → server store→ phone reads + blends → prompt
  • Contract: recall(userId) → { entities, relations, recentObservations, predictions } (+ the Tier-A profile) - maps 1:1 to world_recall. Top-N / salience-capped (≤32KB).
  • The gate is the new confidential→server-readable boundary - reuses the one canonical looks_sensitive screen (all four Q23 zones), drops sensitive entities/observations/predictions + any dangling relation. Tested with a serialized-blob leak check; per-user isolation proven cross-principal.
  • Recipient-parameterized from day one (Recipient::OwnDevices today) so the future eri-social egress extends the same gate instead of opening a second path.
  • Strangler-fig: the projection blends beside the phone's existing learned_facts (Phase A) → becomes source of truth → those tables demote to cache. Never a second TS brain.
  • Status: crates/ half (boundary + gather + isolation + push capability) done. The live trigger + the phone read/blend are the coordinated finish with the phone window (endpoint: superset vs sibling). Files: crates/eri_inference/src/memory/recall.rs; contract docs/contracts/recall-projection-contract.md.

9Phone eri & two-tier memory M1 live

The same eri, reached by texting a phone number (iMessage via Spectrum / Photon, replacing Sendblue) - the cloud half of one brain, serverless on Vercel, Sonnet for the reply. ADR-0001 (memory: adopt patterns not a Python engine; two-tier sync), ADR-0002 (transport + polled command queue).

Tier-A

Personalization projection server-readable

A small (≤32KB) projection - static facts + preference rollups + live dynamic_context - the phone injects to sound like your eri. Encrypted at rest with K_server (defense-in-depth, not E2E). The recall seam extends this to carry the world-model slice. personalization_profiles + /api/v1/memory/profile.

Tier-B

Memory blob E2E

Your full observation log, E2E-encrypted with K_user (HKDF over a stored per-user salt, stable across token rotation). The cloud holds only opaque bytes. Deep/private questions relay to the running computer eri, which holds the keys. memory_blobs + /api/v1/memory/sync.

The sync seam (crates/eri_inference/src/memory/): MemorySync over a SyncTransport trait round-trips the encrypted blob; apply_merge = union + last-write-wins by ts_ms + tombstones (a /forget can't resurrect from a stale copy). Crypto is pure-Rust RustCrypto (aes-gcm/hkdf/sha2), offline-capable.

Honest gap (being closed): today the phone reply reads only shallow TS stores (learned_facts + personalization_profiles) - it does not yet read the Rust world model. The recall seam is the bridge; until its live trigger + read/blend land, eri-on-phone is a personalized Sonnet, not yet the full P1-P3 brain. (M1 hygiene: Photon webhook 401 dedup.)

10Build - eri drives your coding agents shipped

The old "agentic AI IDE" is gone (ADR-0009). eri is a computer-use agent, so it doesn't need its own editor - it orchestrates the agentic-dev tools you already use. You log in to whatever you code with (Claude / Claude Code, Cursor, Gemini, OpenAI, Perplexity); eri drives them.

eri's built-in terminal

Lives inside the app. eri does its coding here - it spins up its own Claude Code sessions, types in your CLI, runs builds and tests, and steers the whole agentic loop. Half the time eri isn't the coder, it's the director of your coder.

eri's own hands shipped

When eri codes directly, it uses the in-house harness (eri_agent / build_agent.rs): tool superset, subagents, compaction, hooks, sandbox, MCP-as-tools, permission modes, Python/Node SDK bindings. An internal capability of the brain, not a product surface.

Engines: Claude drives reasoning + the agentic loop; the local model handles cheap low-level steps at zero cloud cost; Mercury 2 stays available for sub-second single-file edits (no longer tied to an IDE panel). Same idea extends to any app - eri drives your terminal, your browser, your tools; computer use is the substrate (project_eri_coding_control).

13Auth

Web - Google OAuth + JWT

Google OAuth → short-lived JWT access token + single-use rotating refresh token. Client: website/lib/auth-client.ts (single-flight refreshSession). Server verify: userFromBearer() in website/lib/auth.ts - JWT only.

CLI / device - RFC 8628 Device Authorization Grant

Race-proof flow with two distinct codes: device_code (256-bit secret, only the polling client holds it, the token-endpoint key) and user_code (XXXX-XXXX Crockford base32, shown in the browser, can't redeem the token). /poll returns the token idempotently until the 10-min grant expires (kills the single-delivery race). Routes: app/api/v1/auth/device/{start,approve,poll}; CLI: crates/eri_cli/src/auth.rs. Inference verify uses authenticateApiBearer() (accepts eri_sk_), distinct from the JWT verifier.

14Billing & economics

Stripe webhook → sets users.tier → applyTierToBucket() resizes the cap. Real tiers (project_eri_pricing_and_spectrum): Free $0 (Mac-only), Starter $15 (connect apps + phone), Pro $30 (hands-free, multi-model, MCP), Pro+ $60 (codebase training, private brain, 256K). Teams exists in Stripe but is kept off the cards (no teams yet). Metered = opt-in + eri credits.

How eri counts tokens

Every request hits our master key, so we pay the provider; the bucket budget bounds consumption = bounds our cost. Unit today = 1 raw provider token (in or out), counted 1:1. Actual usage from the provider's usage object (Anthropic message_delta.usage off the SSE stream). Mercury is turn-metered (bill:false).

The Pro+ risk: counting in+out 1:1 ignores Sonnet's 5× output multiplier, so an output-heavy Pro+ user can erode margin. Mitigations (priority order): (1) weighted "eri credits" - deduct tokens_in + W×tokens_out (W ~3-5); (2) prompt caching on Claude (~90% off re-sent input - the biggest build saving); (3) token-frugal routing (fast-lane + local model + Mercury keep cloud tokens for real reasoning); (4) hard output/turn caps per run.

15Inference

The metered proxy is the heart and is production-grade. website/lib/inference/proxy.ts - each /api/v1/inference/<verb> is a one-liner calling runProxy: auths the api_token, forwards the body verbatim (Anthropic tools/system/messages/stream pass through - tool-use works), cost-guards (blocks Opus on the shared proxy), quota-gates (402 + ghost), injects the master key, captures real usage from the SSE stream. BYOK (x-eri-byok) skips quota/billing.

Routing (Rust brain)

Tiered, token-frugal (project_eri_token_router): Tier-0 deterministic fast-lane (shipped) → Tier-1 small local classifier → Tier-2 cloud-minimized with a per-tier context window. The ControlTurn / BuildTurn seams let Claude and the local model both drive a loop, with local→Claude escalation. crates/eri_router, crates/eri_cli/src/control_turn.rs.

16eri social - vision grilling in design

The expansion bet: your digital half has a presence, and eris connect on your behalf - a social network for engineered relative intelligence. Not dating, not hookup; a place people unite through their digital imprint. The whitespace nobody's capitalized on.

The architectural insight that makes it fit: the social layer is the same recall-projection primitive, aimed outward (at other people / other eris) instead of at your own phone. The world model is already ~80% of the substrate. What's genuinely new is (1) a consented-egress contract - the loyalty boundary extends from "your devices only" to explicitly-consented recipients, per-item, opt-in, never automatic, revocable; (2) an eri-to-eri protocol, each half loyal to its own owner; (3) autonomous-action gating - any public post is irreversible + outward, so it's always-ask, always-disclosed-as-AI, never impersonating a human.
  • Non-negotiable: the confidential core never leaves; privacy zones never egress; the projection is recipient-parameterized so this extends the existing gate, not a second egress path.
  • Sequencing: post-launch, vision layer. For YC it's the network-effect moat / "where this goes", not the wedge - focus wins the app, the network multiplies the vision.
  • Designed in its own grill (/grill-with-docs); will land as its own ADR + vision doc; do not pull into the current build.
